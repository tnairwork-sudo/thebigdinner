The Tariff

Refund and Cancellation Policy

  1. The tariff is non-refundable once paid, because it is committed to the venue.
  2. Places are first come, first accepted. If a payment arrives after the room is full, it is refunded in full.
  3. If The Big Dinner cancels an edition, the tariff is refunded in full.
  4. Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 7 working days.
  5. A guest cannot cancel for a refund. A guest may ask the host to transfer their place. A transfer is at the host’s discretion.
  6. There is no shipping. This is an in-person gathering in a room.
  7. Correspondence is with the host, at host@thebigdinner.in.
