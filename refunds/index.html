Refunds

  1. The tariff is not returned once paid, because it is committed to the evening.
  2. Places are offered in the order they are accepted. If a payment arrives after the room is full, it is returned in full.
  3. If The Big Dinner cancels an edition, the tariff is returned in full.
  4. A return is sent to the original payment method within 7 working days.
  5. A guest cannot cancel for a return. A guest may ask the host to transfer their place. A transfer is at the host's discretion.
  6. There is no shipping. This is an in-person gathering in a room.
  7. Correspondence is with the host, at host@thebigdinner.in.
