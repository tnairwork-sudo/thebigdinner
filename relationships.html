Relationships

Relationships are the quiet architecture of a life. Most of us know that people change its course. We say so easily, and then leave the work unattended.

What limits a person is rarely circumstance alone. It is the belief that for one to gain, another must lose. The opposite has been truer here: making something with the people around you, until ambition becomes shared.

The Big Dinner began without a map. Only a picture of the room. It is taking shape slowly, and with care.
