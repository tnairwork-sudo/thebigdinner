Terms

  1. The Big Dinner is a private, closed room, a bar, convened monthly in different cities and based in New Delhi, India.
  2. A place is offered by invitation only. The host decides who enters.
  3. The tariff, currently ₹5,000 per guest unless the invitation says otherwise, secures the room. Food and drinks are settled by each guest.
  4. Guests answer an invitation by the date on it. Silence may withdraw the invitation.
  5. Dress is the dress on the invitation: a suit for men, cocktail attire for women.
  6. It is not a networking event, and it is not an occasion for solicitation.
  7. The host may refuse entry, or ask a guest to leave, for conduct.
  8. Payments are processed by third parties.
  9. Indian law governs these terms. The courts at New Delhi have jurisdiction.
  10. Correspondence is with the host, at host@thebigdinner.in.
