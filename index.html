The Philosophy

This is not a
networking event.

The Big Dinner is a closed room. Convened privately, once each month. Every guest is chosen with intention, for the texture of their thinking, the weight of their experience, and the quality of their presence.

No pitches. No elevator lines. No business cards. What happens instead is rarer: conversation that moves, ideas that collide, and friendships that do not require a transaction to begin.

This is how we used to do it. Before the world taught us to be useful before we were real.

The Standard

What the room
requires of you

  1. 01

    Selection

    Participation is extended by invitation only. Every name in this room is chosen deliberately. Our criteria are not disclosed.

  2. 02

    Dress

    Men: a suit. Women: cocktail attire. No exceptions. The room has an aesthetic. You are part of it the moment you walk in.

  3. 03

    RSVP

    Confirm or decline. Ghosting is a choice, and it will be treated as one. Silence results in permanent removal from all cities.

  4. 04

    Participation Fee

    A participation fee applies. It is the filter that separates those who understand the value of a room from those who do not.

  5. 05

    The Evening

    Food and beverages are settled individually with the venue. You are among equals. The conversation is the hospitality.

  6. 06

    Conduct

    The quality of the evening is only ever as good as the people in the room. This is how we protect that.

Before you begin

Social media has taken our focus away. This form is an attempt to restore it.

Please do not fill it as a formality. We receive over a thousand applications in the first week, and we read for self-awareness, clarity, and a story worth hearing.

Dress

Gentlemen arrive in a well-cut suit, tie optional, shoes polished. Ladies arrive in cocktail attire, the kind of dress you'd choose for a grand evening at the bar. Come as you would want to be remembered.

If accepted, there is a tariff of ₹5,000. It secures the venue and is an intended filter.

Every invitation carries an RSVP deadline. Those who do not reply, yes or no, in time are not invited again, in any city.
